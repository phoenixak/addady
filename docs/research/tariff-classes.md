# Research: tariffs for every customer class (commercial, industrial, irrigation, lighting)

- Researched: 2026-10-21. Same rules as `tariff.md`: the regulator first, then official examples, then 2+ agreeing outlets.
- Prices are in piasters per kWh.
- Primary sources: EgyptERA's tariff pages
  - [April 2026 (EN)](https://egyptera.org/en/TarrifApril2026.aspx)
  - [Aug 2024 – Apr 2026 (AR)](https://egyptera.org/ar/TarrifAug2024.aspx)
  - [Jan – Jul 2024 (EN)](https://egyptera.org/en/Tarrif2024N.aspx)

## 1. Commercial (shops, offices, companies on low voltage) — high
The regulator uses the same "consumption band" wording as for homes. The month's total kWh picks the band, and every kWh is priced from zero inside it.

| Month total | Rule | 2024-08 | 2026-04 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0–100 | flat on all kWh | 85 | **162** |
| 101–250 | "من 0-250", flat on all kWh | 168 | **216** |
| 251–1000 | progressive from zero: 0–600, then 601–1000 | 220 / 227 | **264 / 274** |
| > 1000 | "من 0 لأكثر من 1000", flat on all kWh | 233 | **279** |

- **Customer-service fee (EGP/month):** 0–100: 5; 101–250: 15; 251–600: 20; 601–1000: 25; >1000: 40; zero read/closed: 9.
- **Consumption stamp:** **3 piasters per kWh** for every consumer except homes and industry, i.e. commercial and lighting uses. **Annual supply stamp:** 3 EGP, on contracting and every January.
- **August 2026:** no source reports a commercial change. The ministry spokesman said the non-residential increases had applied since April ([Mada Masr](https://www.madamasr.com/2026/05/14/news/%D8%A7%D9%82%D8%AA%D8%B5%D8%A7%D8%AF/%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AD%D9%83%D9%88%D9%85%D8%A9-%D8%AA%D8%B3%D8%AA%D9%83%D9%85%D9%84-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%A5%D8%B9%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%86-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D8%B1%D9%8A-%D8%B9%D9%86-%D8%B2%D9%8A%D8%A7/)), and the August reports cover household tiers only.
  - Verdict: **2026-04 prices still apply; medium confidence.** That rests on the absence of any report of a change.
- Corroboration of the April prices: [Elbalad](https://www.elbalad.news/7007770) and [Youm7](https://www.youm7.com/story/2026/4/4/%D8%AA%D8%B9%D8%B1%D9%81-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%89-%D8%A3%D8%B3%D8%B9%D8%A7%D8%B1-%D8%B4%D8%B1%D8%A7%D8%A6%D8%AD-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%83%D9%87%D8%B1%D8%A8%D8%A7%D8%A1-%D9%84%D9%84%D9%82%D8%B7%D8%A7%D8%B9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AA%D8%AC%D8%A7%D8%B1%D9%8A-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%AC%D8%AF%D9%8A%D8%AF%D8%A9-%D8%A8%D9%86%D8%B3%D8%A8%D8%A9-20/7366270) give 216 / 264 / 274 / 279, applied from the May bill (April consumption). One outlet printed "324" for 0–600, which is a typo; the regulator says 264.

## 2. Voltage-level customers (factories, large companies, utilities) — high
One energy price per kWh, flat on all consumption.

| Voltage | Users | 2024-08 | 2026-04 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Extra-high 220–132 kV | KIMA / Metro / others | 160 / 160 / 160 | 189 / 189 / 189 (grid-linked distribution companies 184) |
| High 66–33 kV | Metro / others | 174 / 174 | 205 / 205 (distribution companies 200) |
| Medium 22–11 kV | irrigation / water & sewage / others | 194 / 194 / 194 | 255 / 255 / 255 (distribution companies 250) |
| Low 380 V | irrigation / others / public lighting | 200 / 234 / 234 | 265 / 274 / 274 |

- **Customer-service fee:** 35 EGP/month for extra-high, high and medium voltage; 4 EGP for low-voltage irrigation; 15 EGP for other low-voltage uses.
- **Industrial consumption stamp:** **0.6 piasters per 10 kWh** for non-lighting uses.
- **Industrial discount cancelled:** a prime-ministerial decision cancelled decree 781/2020, which gave a 10 pt/kWh discount to industrial activity on extra-high, high and medium voltage, **effective 1 July 2025** ([Egyin](https://www.egyin.com/article/52325/)).
  - Low confidence on whether the 2026-04 prices already net this out; the regulator's table shows a single price.

### Demand charge and time-of-use (history)
- Until July 2024 ([EgyptERA Jan 2024](https://egyptera.org/en/Tarrif2024N.aspx)), large customers paid a **demand charge (EGP/kW/month)** of 40 (extra-high), 50 (high) and 60 (medium voltage), plus **on-peak/off-peak** energy prices (e.g. extra-high "other users": average 126.9, off-peak 117.1, on-peak 175.7).
- From August 2024 the regulator's tables list a **single energy price** with no demand-charge column.
- The ministry's industrial guide still has a "مقابل القدرة (جنيه/ك.و)" column in its bill template ([MoEE guide](http://www.moee.gov.eg/test_new/DOC/ad1(1).pdf)).
- **Engine decision:** price factories as energy + service fee + stamps, and let the user add a demand-charge line read from their own bill.

## 3. Power factor — threshold high, formula low
- **Official:** "تشترط شركات توزيع الكهرباء على أن لا يقل متوسط معامل القدرة السنوى عن 0.92 وتلزم المصانع بتركيب مكثفات لتحسين معامل القدرة مما يساهم بتوفير من 5 - 15% في فاتورة الكهرباء" (distribution companies require an annual average power factor of at least 0.92 and oblige factories to install capacitors, which saves 5–15% on the bill). Source: [MoEE industrial energy-efficiency guide](http://www.moee.gov.eg/test_new/DOC/ad1(1).pdf), §4. The tariff itself states that "prices are applied based on a power factor of 0.92".
- **The penalty formula is unpublished in official sources found.** A 2020 technical blog describes the penalty on the annual average power factor for contracted load above 500 kW:
  - between 0.7 and 0.9: (0.9 − pf) × kWh
  - an incentive between 0.92 and 0.95
- That's a single, old, unofficial source, so **low confidence**. The app shows it as an *estimate* and recommends reaching 0.92 with capacitors, which is physics and certain.
- **Law 87/2015, Art. 48:** a customer whose contracted capacity exceeds **500 kW** must appoint an energy-efficiency officer (MoEE guide, §1).

## 4. Other unified prices
- **Coded meters:** flat 274 pt/kWh since April 2026, previously 214 ([Masrawy](https://www.masrawy.com/news/news_egypt/details/2026/4/8/2969171)). The arrears that appeared on top-up receipts were investigated by the Consumer Protection Agency ([Al Manassa](https://almanassa.com/en/news/31814)).
- **Stolen-power settlements** are billed at a unified cost rate: 214.5 pt in Aug 2024 ([Al Arabiya](https://www.alarabiya.net/aswaq/exclusive/2024/08/26/)).
