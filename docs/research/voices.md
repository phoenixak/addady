# Research: what people struggle with → what Addady builds

- Researched: 2026-10-21 from news coverage of complaints, official explanations and app-store reviews.
- A ranked list; each need maps to a feature.

| # | Pain point (as people say it) | Evidence | Feature |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **"ليه رصيدي اتخصم؟"** (why did my balance get deducted?) Money vanishes at recharge: debts, "arrears" | Consumer Protection Agency probe of coded-meter arrears ([Al Manassa](https://almanassa.com/en/news/31814)); deductions explained ([El Watan](https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8333518)); MPs asking "where did the card balance go" ([Parlmany](https://www.parlmany.com/News/2/557365/)) | **Recharge explainer:** enter the amount charged and the balance after, and the app itemises the likely deductions (grace debt, tier difference, fees, price differences) |
| 2 | **"الفاتورة جات عالية فجأة"** (the bill suddenly came in high) | sudden jumps without new appliances ([Dostor](https://www.dostor.org/5683848)) | Bill checker (exists) plus "was it an estimated reading?" and the cliff explainer |
| 3 | **Estimated readings** | ([Dostor](https://www.dostor.org/5683852)) | A self-reading reminder on the company's reading days, plus how to self-read |
| 4 | **Prepaid cut without warning** | the most common cause of cuts ([El Balad](https://www.elbalad.news/7007768)) | Run-out prediction (exists) plus a holiday calendar |
| 5 | **Don't know my tier / prices changed again** | conflicting news tables (`tariff.md`) | Tier staircase plus signed tariff updates |
| 6 | **Where do I complain? What are my rights?** | procedures scattered across news articles | **اعرف حقك** (know your rights): step cards, 121, a letter template |
| 7 | **Official apps don't work** | Apple review: "spent half an hour describing a fault, the app didn't send it" ([App Store](https://apple.co/3EcoZrC)) | Offline-first, no account. The app never pretends to submit to the government; it hands off to the official channel |
| 8 | **Shops and businesses: is my commercial bill right?** | commercial prices rose 20–90% in April 2026 | Commercial class with stamps and fees |
| 9 | **Factories: power factor charges, high cost** | MoEE guide: 5–15% saving from capacitors | Power factor and kvar tool (v0.3) |
| 10 | **Outages and backup** | load-shedding fear every summer ([Akhbar El Yom](https://akhbarelyom.com/news/newdetails/4837989/1/)) | UPS/generator sizing (v0.4) |
