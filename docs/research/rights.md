# Research: consumer rights and procedures (for "اعرف حقك")

- Researched: 2026-10-21. Procedures change, so every card in the app shows its source and "check with your distribution company".

| Topic | What sources say | Confidence | Sources |
|---|---|---|---|
| Report a wrong reading, outage or fault | Call **121** (unified hotline; a technician visits within ~48 h) or the distribution company office. Escalate unresolved complaints to **EgyptERA** (Complaints@egyptera.org) | medium-high | [Dostor](https://www.dostor.org/5683848), [EgyptERA contact](https://egyptera.org/en/TarrifApril2026.aspx) |
| Contest a bill | Written request at the distribution company's commercial department, with copies of bills and receipts. Common grounds: estimated reading, wrong tier month, meter fault | medium | [El Watan](https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8376821), [Dostor](https://www.dostor.org/5683848) |
| Meter test | Written request plus a test fee. The meter is tested at the central lab; **if faulty, the fee is refunded and overcharges are settled** (as credit on later bills) | medium (fee amount unknown) | [Dostor](https://www.dostor.org/5683848), [Banker](https://www.banker.news/156155) |
| Avoid estimated bills | Self-read on the unified e-services platform: type the digits exactly as shown and confirm | medium | [Dostor](https://www.dostor.org/5683852) |
| Late payment | **7%** charge after a grace period of up to **30 days from receiving** the bill (not from its issue date). It applies to postpaid bills only. Continued non-payment can lead to the meter being replaced with a prepaid one; sources say 1–2 months | medium | [Al Mal](https://almalnews.com/2139113/) |
| Installment of arrears | Not automatic; studied case by case; reports of 6–12 months | low-medium | [El Watan](https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8333415) |
| Prepaid deductions at recharge | Legitimate causes: **grace-period debt**, **tier differences** (the month is repriced when you cross a tier), accumulated **service fees**, **retroactive price changes**, meter instalments, emergency credit. The red lamp shows at 10 EGP | medium-high | [El Watan](https://www.elwatannews.com/news/details/8333518), [Parlmany](https://www.parlmany.com/News/2/557365/) |
| Coded → legal meter | ID; ownership or registered lease; last receipt; a certificate of legalisation or acceptance of reconciliation (**Form 8**). Buildings from **before 1975** only need proof of age. Apply on the distribution company platform; a technical inspection follows. The meter itself usually stays | medium | [Masrawy](https://www.masrawy.com/news/news_egypt/details/2026/4/20/2975195/), [Dostor](https://www.dostor.org/5518671) |
| New meter | Apply on the unified e-services platform (account, documents). Old published prices for a single-phase meter: ~1274 EGP plus 285 installation (2023, Beheira) | low (prices outdated) | [Al Mal](https://almalnews.com/2140324/) |
| Tampering / theft | Contract termination, a police report, prison and large fines. **Figures conflict:** 6 months + 10k–100k EGP versus a 2024 amendment of ≥1 year + 100k–1M EGP, plus reconciliation at 2× to 4× the value taken. **The app never gives tampering tips**; it only explains the legal risk | medium (exact figures disputed) | [Masrawy](https://www.masrawy.com/news/news_egypt/details/2024/11/20/2678215/), [Dostor](https://www.dostor.org/5507046) |
